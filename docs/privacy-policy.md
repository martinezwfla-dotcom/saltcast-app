# Saltcast Florida privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

The Saltcast Florida app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** You don't sign up or log in.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app has no ads, analytics, or tracking tools.
- **Your settings stay on your phone.** Your favorite spots, chosen activity, and units are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
- **Location.** The app doesn't use your location. You pick spots from a list.
- **Weather data.** To show forecasts, the app asks Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com), NOAA (tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov), and Windy (windy.com) for data about the spot you picked. Those services see your device's IP address, as with any web request, under their own privacy policies. The app sends them no information about you.

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.

Questions? Contact us through saltcastflorida.com.
