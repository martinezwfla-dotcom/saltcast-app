**Description:**
Saltcast Florida tells you whether it's a good time to get out on the water, and when the best time is.

Pick your activity, whether it's fishing, surfing, diving and snorkeling, boating, sailing, or kayak and SUP, and a spot anywhere from Pensacola to Key West. Saltcast checks the wind, gusts, seas, swell, rain, and storms, and gives you a simple Go, Maybe, or No Go.

- The best windows in the next 48 hours, ranked
- Wind, gusts, seas, swell, and water temperature
- High and low tides from NOAA
- Live radar
- 21 spots across the Gulf, Keys, Atlantic, and Panhandle
- Save your favorite spots
- Dock Talk, with local tips and the seasonal outlook

Verdicts are rules of thumb, not official guidance. Always check the National Weather Service marine forecast and use your own judgment on the water.
